While something loads

The biggest visible change. Before, a slow reply blanked the page or swapped it for grey blocks; now the page stays and only the part that is waiting changes.

1

Filters and pages keep the old rows up, dimmed

Orders, Customers, Complaints, Shifts, Users, Campaigns, Queue, a Customer's Orders, Reports panels and Aging Queue

blink → 0
BeforeAny chip, filter, search or page change waited a second, then swapped the whole page (title, filters, the search box you were typing in) for three grey blocks, then drew it again.
NowThe page never leaves. The old rows fade to 60% until the new ones arrive, then the new rows fade in over 0.2s. The status counts keep their old numbers meanwhile.

Files: lib/content-fade.ts, components/data-table.tsx, data-table-grid.tsx, data-table-cards.tsx; the loaders in routes/_admin/orders.index, customers.index, customers.$customerId, complaints.index, users, shifts and reports skip the wait on a filter change; queue.index keeps the old cards the same way. Audit ids O1, O2, O13, W5, W10, R8, V1, V2, V4.

Trade-off: while the old rows are dimmed they are the old answer and can still be tapped. If a reply never came the list would stay dimmed; that happened once in testing after a fast run of filter taps and could not be repeated.

Reply: keep / change / revert 1

Nested sheet header grows a row

Order sheet on a laptop → Collect payment (any nested sheet)

0.021
What you seeThe Order sheet's header is one row (arrows, "Order 1 of 25", Open full page, close). The nested sheet's header adds a back-link row ("‹ #BGR/19102026/3") above its title, so the body starts 54px lower than the Order's did.
AlsoIn Collect payment, "No campaigns available" shows first, then the campaign tiles land and the box grows 94 → 126px, pushing the form down (0.003 on a laptop, 0.006 on a phone). That part is a small fix whichever option you pick.

A. Always keep the back-link row

Every sheet reserves the row, empty when there is nothing to go back to. Simplest; costs 54px of blank space at the top of every plain sheet.

B. One row for back link and actions

The back link takes the place of the arrows and "Order 1 of 25" on the same row, so both headers are the same height. No new space, no motion. The row gets tight on a narrow sheet. My pick, since new animation is on hold.

C. Slide the nested sheet in as a panel

Collect payment slides in over the Order like an iPhone push, so the new header reads as a new screen, not a jump. Most work, and adds an animation.

Files: components/ui/global-sheet.tsx (header), features/transactions/components/campaign-tile-group.tsx (campaigns). Score 0.0033 counted + 0.0176 counted as your click on a laptop.
