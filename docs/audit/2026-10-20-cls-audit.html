By cause

Most findings share a handful of causes. Fixing a cause fixes every finding under it.

1. Slow loads swap the whole page for a grey placeholder

biggest blink

On list pages, every filter, search, chip or page change re-runs the route's loader and waits for it. After one second the router replaces the entire page — title, filters, the search box you're typing in, the table — with the same three grey blocks, then swaps the page back. On prod's slower database this happens often.
